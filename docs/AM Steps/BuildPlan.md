# 9. Build AM Plan

Now that all the steps have been completed the Asset Management Plan can be compiled!

As mentioned earlier, the AM plan summarizes the plans to achieve the established objectives for each asset type, including: the state of the infrastructure, desired level of service, lifecycle management strategy and financial strategy. Stemming from it are detailed plans for maintenance and capital projects. These should be updated in a short time frame (1 year) and the broader AM plan in the medium term (5 years)
 [(FCM 2018)](https://fcm.ca/en/resources/mamp/guidebook-how-develop-asset-management-policy-and-strategy). All supporting data should be continuously updated as new inspections, studies and projects are conducted.
